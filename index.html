This Privacy Policy describes how our application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our services. The App is a utility toolbox that provides a compass, bubble level, noise meter, flashlight, and travel checklist features.

The App integrates the following third-party service:

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information. All data transmitted to our advertising partner is done over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
